## Provenance and limitations

- Census: downloaded the official vintage-2025 resident single-year age CSV. The manifest records source URL, retrieval date, SHA-256 and exact filters. Totals are recomputed by the model, not copied from a secondary TAM report.
- Market evidence combines different dates and survey universes; the noninvestor proxy is explicitly approximate. Cash App accounts and crypto-ever-use are not unique funded investing prospects. SAM filters, acquisition funnels, commercial costs and behavior remain assumptions.
- Cash App stablecoin help used the official HTML content because the search tool did not extract the article body. Published limits, networks and exclusions were read from that content. Account-level eligibility and private APIs remain unverified.
- SEC September 2026 tokenization analysis uses the operative 60-page order (Release 34-106402), not only the press release. The April interface statement remains staff guidance; neither is treated as blanket approval.
- Provider documentation can differ between sandbox, international products and contracted US programs. A published chain list or API fee parameter is not evidence of program availability or lawful fee entitlement.
- App policy and SDK requirements reflect the research date. Recheck the official page and console before release, including any account-specific deadline or exemption.
- No signed provider quote, legal opinion, counsel engagement, app-console state, bank account, entity/license filing or trademark search result was supplied or independently verified. These are explicit closure gates.
- No customer survey/interviews, campaign experiments, live funded cohorts, device acceptance or production transaction were conducted. Numerical scenarios are not predictions and have no assigned probability.
- Local source review and browser smoke establish prototype behavior only. Screenshots are synthetic account data, not customer records.
- Link availability and public text may change. Preserve approved contracts, dated decisions and release evidence in access-controlled company records; refresh this dossier when the product structure changes.
