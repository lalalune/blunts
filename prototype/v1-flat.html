How it works
  1. You drop cashFrom Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, a debit card or your bank.
  2. We buy the sliceYour money buys the whole mix above, down to fractions of a share.
  3. It grows (or dips)Stocks move every day. Long run, big tech has gone up, but there are down years.
  4. Cash out anytimeNo lockups. Tax stash handled on the way out.
How we get paid

Can I lose money?

Yes. If the stocks drop, your worth drops below what you put in. Your blunts still show what you put in so you can see the difference. Don't put in money you need for rent.

Where is my money held?

With a regulated custodian that holds the actual stocks for you. Blunts can't spend it. (Partner TBD in prototype.)

Why "blunts"?

It's just how we count. $100 is a blunt, $1,000 is a band. There's no weed involved.

Design calls

Blunts = money in, never gains. A market dip never "un-rolls" a blunt. Gains live in a separate Grown number.

Tax stash, not withholding. US brokers don't withhold capital-gains tax. We set aside an estimate that stays the user's money and settle it at filing time.

Fee shown at cash-out only. Itemized every time. Toggle the fee model above: profit-share is off-limits for most US retail users, so the prototype shows the flat-fee fallback too.

No crypto words anywhere. Tokens, chains and wallets stay under the hood.
